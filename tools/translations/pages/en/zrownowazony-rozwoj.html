We manufacture in Poland, close to our customers. That means shorter transport, a smaller carbon footprint and full control over how our products are made.

Made to order

We make most of our range only after an order is placed. That way we do not produce for stock and we limit surplus that ends up on sale or is disposed of.

Durable products

We focus on yarns and workmanship that last for many seasons of wear. A hat that looks good for longer means less waste and a better product for your customers.

Local and responsible

We work with a skilled team in our factory near Krakow and grow the company with future seasons in mind. Questions about materials or the process? Write to us.
