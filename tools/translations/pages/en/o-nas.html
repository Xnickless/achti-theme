A Polish hat manufacturer with over 30 years of experience

ACHTI is a Polish manufacturer of winter hats, headbands and balaclavas that has been combining knitting expertise with a modern approach to producing winter accessories for over 30 years. All our products are made in our own production facility near Kraków, where we control every stage – from preparing the knit fabric to finishing the final product.

We create collections for women, men and children, offering both timeless styles and models that follow current trends. Our goal is to supply businesses with products that combine refined design, consistent workmanship and flexible terms of cooperation.

We manufacture for business

We specialise in serving B2B customers. We work with brick-and-mortar and online shops, wholesalers, retail chains, fashion brands and advertising agencies.

Our products reach customers in Poland, the European Union and other markets abroad.

The ACHTI B2B platform lets businesses explore our collection, browse the models and place orders at wholesale prices.

Our own production. More flexibility.

Our own production facility allows us to manage order fulfilment directly and respond to the needs of business customers.
