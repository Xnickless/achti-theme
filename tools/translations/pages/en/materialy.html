We choose yarns so that a hat is warm, soft and keeps its shape for many seasons. You will find the full composition of each model in the “Composition” tab on the product page.

What our hats are made of

  • Acrylic — the basis of most models: light, warm, pilling-resistant and easy to care for.
  • Wool and merino wool — in models marked “With wool” or “Merino wool”; naturally warm and breathable. Merino models are in the Premium Merino collection.
  • Polyamide, polyester, nylon, viscose, elastane — in blends they add durability, stretch and softness to the knit.

Lining

Many models have a full fleece lining or an inner fleece band that protects the ears and forehead from the wind. Use the “Features” filter in any collection to choose lined or unlined hats.
