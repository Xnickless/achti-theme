2. What cookies are

Cookies are small text files stored on the user's device while using the platform. Similar technologies (e.g. the browser's localStorage) work in the same way and are covered by this Policy. Under Article 399 of the Polish Electronic Communications Law of 12 July 2024, storing or accessing information on the user's device requires consent, except for files that are necessary to provide the service requested by the user.

4. Managing consent

On the first visit, a banner is displayed in which you can accept all files, reject optional files or choose categories. Not giving consent does not restrict access to the platform or the ability to place orders. Consent can be changed or withdrawn at any time – after deleting cookies in your browser, the banner will be shown again on your next visit. Cookies can also be blocked in the browser settings; blocking necessary files may prevent logging in and placing orders.
