1. What cookies are

Cookies are small text files stored on the user’s device while using the platform. Similar technologies (e.g. localStorage, pixels) work on the same principle and are covered by this Policy. Under Art. 399 of the Polish Electronic Communications Law of 12 July 2024, storing information on the user’s device or accessing it requires consent, except for files necessary to provide a service requested by the user.

3. Managing consent

On the first visit a banner is displayed in which you can accept all files, reject optional files or select categories. Refusing consent does not limit access to the platform or the ability to place orders. Consent can be changed or withdrawn at any time by clicking “Cookie preferences” in the page footer. Cookies can also be deleted or blocked in the browser settings; blocking necessary files may prevent logging in and placing orders.

4. Personal data

Information collected by cookies (IP address, identifiers) may constitute personal data. The rules for processing it, including transfers to providers outside the EEA, are described in the Privacy Policy.
