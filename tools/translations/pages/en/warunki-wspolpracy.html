Achti is a Polish manufacturer of winter hats and scarves for women, men and children, with more than 30 years of experience. All production takes place in our own factory near Krakow. The B2B platform is used for wholesale orders by shops, retail chains, distributors and companies ordering promotional hats.

Who can buy

Businesses only: brick-and-mortar and online shops, wholesalers, chains, advertising agencies and other businesses from Poland, the European Union and other countries worldwide. We do not sell to consumers. Registration requires a Polish NIP or an EU VAT number. For customers from outside the EU, verification may take longer.

Getting started – 3 steps

  1. Registration – fill in the “Company registration” form. We verify your details in the CEIDG/KRS registers and VIES.
  2. Activation – within 1–2 business days we activate your account and send a confirmation email. You sign in with your email address and a one-time code, no password needed.
  3. Ordering – once signed in you see wholesale prices, volume discounts, availability and the quick order table, where you can add several colours of one model at once.

Prices and discounts

  • All prices are net, in PLN; 23% VAT is added in the cart. EU companies with a valid EU VAT number – 0% VAT (intra-community supply).
  • Volume discounts depend on the number of pieces or the order value. The current tiers are shown in the cart at checkout.
  • Minimum order value: PLN 2,000 net. You can order single pieces of a given colour.

Availability and lead times

  • Made to order – our standard range, produced to order in 7–14 business days. When confirming each order we always confirm the exact lead time, counted from the day the prepayment is credited to our account.
  • In stock – stock items, dispatched within 1–3 business days.

Payment

  • A 50% prepayment based on a pro forma invoice, and the remaining 50% when the goods are ready for dispatch.
  • After completed orders, deferred payment terms of 14 or 30 days are possible within an agreed credit limit (set individually).
  • Invoices are issued in KSeF (the Polish national e-invoicing system); we also send a copy by email.

Delivery

Courier delivery in Poland and the EU, pallet shipments for large orders, collection in person in Bieńkowice. Free delivery in Poland from PLN 5,000 net. Details: Shipping and delivery.

Complaints and returns

Goods of full value that match the order cannot be returned or exchanged. Goods may be returned only with the seller’s prior written consent. Any quantity discrepancies between the actual delivery and the quantity on the invoice must be reported within 3 days of delivery at the latest. Parcels showing signs of opening or damage must be checked in the presence of the courier, and a damage report must be drawn up on the form the courier always carries. Details: Returns and complaints.

Private label and promotional hats

We make hats with your logo (embroidery, patch, sewn-in label, tag) as well as exclusive products under your own brand. Minimum 100 pcs per model and colour, with the design approved before production. Configure a hat and request a quote under Promotional hats, and choose models for your own brand under Private Label.

Materials for shops

Once signed in, you can use our product photos and descriptions to sell in your shop. We also provide an XML product feed for integration with online shops – write to us to get the link.
