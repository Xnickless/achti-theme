Achti is a Polish manufacturer of hats, snoods and balaclavas with over 30 years of experience. All production takes place at our factory near Kraków. The B2B platform is used for placing wholesale orders by stores, retail chains, distributors and companies ordering promotional hats.

Who can buy

Businesses only: brick-and-mortar and online stores, wholesalers, chains, advertising agencies and other entrepreneurs from Poland and the European Union. We do not sell to consumers. Registration requires a Polish tax ID (NIP) or an EU VAT number.

How to start – 3 steps

  1. Registration – fill in the “Company registration” form. We verify the data in the CEIDG/KRS registers and VIES.
  2. Activation – within [1–2] working days we activate the account and send a confirmation e-mail. You log in with your e-mail address and a one-time code, no password.
  3. Ordering – once logged in you see wholesale prices, discount tiers, availability and the quick order table, where you can add several colours of one model at once.
