This page sets out all pricing rules of the Achti B2B wholesale platform: prices, minimum order, volume discounts, delivery and payment.

Prices

  • All prices on the platform are wholesale prices excluding VAT. VAT is added in the cart: 23% for Polish companies, reverse charge for EU companies with a valid EU VAT number.
  • Prices are visible only to registered and approved companies. Register your company.
  • We invoice in PLN (Poland), EUR (European Union) or USD (other countries).

Minimum order

  • 2000 PLN net (470 EUR / 550 USD) per order.
  • At least 2 pcs of each colour.

Delivery

  • Poland: 20 PLN gross for every started 50 pcs, free delivery from 5000 PLN net.
  • Other countries: delivery cost is shown in the cart and at checkout once you select your country.

Payment

100% prepayment before the order is processed.
