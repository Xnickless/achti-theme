Returns and complaints

Quality complaints

We are the manufacturer and we stand behind the quality of every piece. If a product has a manufacturing defect (e.g. a knitting fault, a faulty seam, a patch coming off, a colour or size not matching the order), file a complaint:

  1. When: defects visible on receipt (quantity, colour, size, damage) – within 7 days of delivery; hidden defects – within 14 days of discovery, but no later than 12 months from delivery.
  2. How: by e-mail to sprzedaz@achti.pl with the order or invoice number, the product code (SKU), the number of pieces concerned, a description of the defect and photos. You can also use the contact form.
  3. What next: we reply within 14 days. If we ask you to send the goods back, we cover the shipping cost (we send a return label). An accepted complaint is settled by replacement with a defect-free product, repair, a price reduction or a refund (credit note and bank transfer within 14 days).

What a complaint does not cover: natural shade differences between yarn batches, minor differences resulting from hand finishing, wear and tear during use, mechanical damage after delivery and the effects of washing contrary to the care label.

Transport damage

Record any damage to the shipment in a damage report with the courier and send us a scan of it with photos within 7 days of receipt. We will handle the claim with the carrier and send the missing or damaged goods. Details on the Shipping and delivery page.

Returns of non-defective goods

Sales on the platform are wholesale (B2B), so the statutory 14-day right of withdrawal provided for consumers does not apply. We understand, however, that stocking a store can be hard to predict, therefore:

  • we accept returns of non-defective products from the current collection, unused, with tags, within 30 days of delivery – after prior agreement by e-mail;
  • the cost of return shipping is borne by the buyer; we deduct a handling fee of [X% / no fee – to be decided];
  • the price is refunded by credit note and bank transfer within 14 days of receiving the goods;
  • we do not accept returns of private label products, hats with the customer’s embroidery or patch, or other products made to individual order.

Instead of a return we will gladly offer an exchange for other models or colours from the current range.

Order cancellation

An order can be cancelled free of charge until it is accepted for processing (e-mail confirmation). “Made to order” products can be cancelled after production has started only with our consent; in that case we may charge the cost of the materials used.
