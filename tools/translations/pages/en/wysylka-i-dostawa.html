Shipping and delivery

Lead times

  • Made to order – most of our range is produced to order at our factory in Bieńkowice. Indicative lead time: 7–14 working days from order acceptance (for prepayment – from the date the payment is credited). The exact date is given in the order confirmation.
  • In stock – products marked this way are held in our warehouse; we ship them within [1–3] working days.
  • An order containing both types of products is shipped in full once complete, unless partial shipment is agreed.
  • During peak season (September–December) lead times may be longer. We inform you of any delay by e-mail.
  • Private label and promotional hats: the lead time is agreed individually with the quotation and counted from approval of the design.

Delivery to European Union countries

We ship to all EU countries. The cost depends on the destination country and the weight of the shipment and is shown in the cart once the address is entered. As a guide: [Germany, Czech Republic, Slovakia – PLN XX; other EU countries – PLN XX; pallet shipments – quotation]. Delivery time: 2–5 working days from dispatch.

Companies with a valid EU VAT number receive an invoice with 0% VAT (intra-Community supply of goods). We verify the number in the VIES system when activating the account.

Shipping outside the EU (e.g. United Kingdom, Switzerland, Norway): please contact our export department – eksport@achti.pl. Customs duties and import taxes are paid by the recipient.

Packaging

Hats are packed in bulk cartons; on request each piece can be packed in a separate polybag or labelled for the customer (additional service, individual quotation). If you need a specific packaging method, e.g. for distribution to several stores, please state this in the order notes.

Receiving the shipment

  1. Check the condition of the packaging in the presence of the courier. If the carton is damaged, crushed or wet, draw up a damage report with the courier and take photos.
  2. Check the contents immediately after receipt. Report shortages or damage not visible from the outside within 7 days of receipt (Art. 76 of the Polish Transport Law) to sprzedaz@achti.pl, quoting the order number and attaching photos.
  3. Without a damage report it is difficult to pursue claims against the carrier, so please draw one up whenever damage is visible.

Shipment tracking

Once the shipment is dispatched we send an e-mail with the tracking number. The order status is also visible after logging in under “Orders”.
