Account and access

Why can’t I see prices?
Wholesale prices are visible only to logged-in, verified companies. Register your company and, once the account is activated, log in with your e-mail address.

How long does account activation take?
Usually [1–2] working days. We check the tax ID / EU VAT number in the registers. If you have not heard from us after that time, check your spam folder or write to sprzedaz@achti.pl.

How do I log in? I don’t have a password.
Login works without a password: enter the e-mail address used at registration and you will receive a one-time code. The code is valid for a few minutes.

Can several people from my company place orders?
Yes. Let us know which e-mail addresses to add to the company account. Each person logs in with their own address and the orders go to the shared company account.

I have several stores. Can I have several delivery addresses?
Yes, we can assign multiple locations to one company account. When ordering, you choose where to ship.

Can a private individual buy a hat?
No, the platform is for wholesale sales to businesses only. We will provide a list of stores that carry our products on request.

Range and products

What do “Made to order” and “In stock” mean?
“Made to order” is the standard: we produce after the order is placed, within 7–14 working days. “In stock” means the product is in our warehouse and we ship it within [1–3] days.

How do I order several colours of one model?
On the product page, below the description, you will find the “Quick order” table with all colours of the model. Enter the quantities and add everything to the cart with one click. The colour circles above the description switch the view between colours.

What sizes are available?
Adult hats come in a universal size (One Size, approx. 54–60 cm). Children’s hats come in sizes 50–52 and 52–54 cm. Details in the “Size” tab on the product page.

What are the hats made of?
The fibre composition of each model is given in the “Composition” tab on the product page and on the care label. We use acrylic and wool yarns, blends with alpaca and merino, and fur-effect yarns.

Can I get product photos for my store?
Yes. After logging in you can download photos from the product page. We also provide an XML feed with the whole range and photo packs on request.

Do you make hats with my logo?
Yes: embroidery, patch, woven label or your own tag. Minimum [X] pcs per model and colour. Configure a hat and request a quote in the Private Label section.

Orders and payments

Is there a minimum order?
[Yes, PLN XXX net / There is no logistic minimum.] We do not require bulk packs – you can order single pieces of a colour.

How do quantity discounts work?
The price drops automatically once the quantity or order value threshold is exceeded. The thresholds are shown next to the product after logging in.

What payment methods are available?
Bank transfer against a pro forma invoice, electronic payment in the cart and, for regular customers, bank transfer with deferred payment terms of [14/30] days.

When will I receive the invoice?
The VAT invoice is issued on dispatch in the Polish National e-Invoicing System (KSeF); a copy is also sent by e-mail. EU companies receive an invoice with 0% VAT (intra-Community supply).

Can I change or cancel my order?
Yes, until the order is accepted for processing. Write to sprzedaz@achti.pl as soon as possible, quoting the order number.

Delivery

How much does delivery cost?
Courier in Poland PLN [XX] net, free from PLN [AMOUNT] net. To EU countries – the cost is shown in the cart. Collection in person in Bieńkowice is free. Details: Shipping and delivery.

Do you ship abroad?
Yes, to all EU countries. Outside the EU – after contacting the export department (eksport@achti.pl).

The parcel arrived damaged. What should I do?
Draw up a damage report with the courier, take photos and send them to us within 7 days. We will send the missing goods and handle the claim with the carrier.

Complaints and returns

The product has a defect. How do I complain?
Send an e-mail with the order number, product code, a description and photos. We reply within 14 days; if the complaint is accepted, we replace the goods or refund the money. Details: Returns and complaints.

Can I return goods that did not sell?
Wholesale sales do not carry a statutory right of return, but we accept agreed returns of non-defective goods from the current collection up to 30 days from delivery. We will gladly offer an exchange for other models.
